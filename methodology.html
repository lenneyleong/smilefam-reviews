Methodology

How these reviews are collected — and what we leave out.

This site is operated by SmileFam. Reviews are harvested from Google, Facebook and the SmileFam store’s review app, republished with their source and date, and re-verified against the raw harvest on every update. Last updated 1 Aug 2026.

What is filtered, exactly

Three filters apply, and none of them touches ratings:

  • No-text reviews are skipped. A star with no words cannot be republished as a review. The gap is disclosed in the table above.
  • Store reviews are currently limited to those with customer photos — 136 of the 417the store holds. This is a volume decision, not a rating one: every store review, photo or not, is five stars, so the cut changes no average. The store’s app also holds a number of reviews it collected but never published; those are not on this site either — the exact count is recorded in the raw snapshot metadata committed with this site.
  • Promotional-sounding reviews are demoted, not deleted. 255 reviews read like seeded or affiliate copy — checkmark feature lists, calls to action. They stay in the archive and in the counts, but they are never chosen as featured reviews.

Negative reviews are not filtered, demoted, or held back. All 6 reviews rated 3 stars or lower are published in full and linked from the site header and every review browser.

How you can check we didn’t write these

Most reviews here live on platforms SmileFam cannot edit — Google and Facebook — and every review links to its source listing. Each review also shows a short fingerprint (the grey code in its footer): a cryptographic hash of the review text, computed from the raw data as it arrived from the platform. Before every site update, an automated check re-hashes every review against that raw record and fails the build on any mismatch. A review cannot be added, edited or invented here without breaking its fingerprint.

One honest limitation: Facebook does not provide links to individual reviews, so Facebook reviews link to the page’s review listing rather than the exact review. Google reviews link directly to the individual review on Google Maps. On verification: a small number of store reviews are order-linked — 6 of the 136we hold — and the rest are labelled “submitted to the store — purchase not verified”. Google and Facebook reviews are public and unverifiable, and are labelled exactly that.

Names and photos

Reviewer names are shortened to first name and last initial before they are ever stored — full surnames never reach this site’s records. Profile photos are never collected from any platform. Customer photos are shown only for reviews submitted to the SmileFam store, where customers uploaded them to the brand’s own review app; photos posted on Google or Facebook stay on those platforms. Location data embedded in photo files is stripped before publishing.

Corrections and removals

If you wrote one of these reviews and want it or your photo removed from this site, email info@getsmilefam.com. We remove it, record the removal, and the site’s counts drop accordingly — removals are logged, not silently absorbed. The same address handles factual corrections.

Common questions

Do you remove negative reviews?

No. Every review rated 3 stars or lower that we hold — currently 6 — is published in full at /reviews/critical, unedited.

Who runs this site?

SmileFam Pte Ltd (UEN 202316423M), the company whose products are reviewed. That relationship is disclosed at the top of every page.

How do I get a review corrected or removed?

Email info@getsmilefam.com. If you are the reviewer and want your review or photo removed from this site, we remove it and record the removal — the count on the site will drop accordingly.
