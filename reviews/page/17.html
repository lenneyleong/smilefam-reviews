i’ve been using the electric toothbrush for a few months now and my teeth do feel cleaner compared to using a traditional toothbrush, but i’m not sure if it helps with whitening. it is difficult to gauge when to charge the toothbrush as there is no battery indicator on it (a sure sign is when the toothbrush stops working as you’re brushing your teeth). i’ve also used the whitening kit about once in 2 weeks. i think it would show more results if i were more consistent, but personally it is uncomfortable having the gel and the device in my mouth for 25 minutes (both blue and red light cycle). the gel works better if you blot your teeth with tissue beforehand! as for the battery, i experienced similar issues with both the toothbrush and whitening device. it would be great if there was a battery indicator to alert us to charge before using the device. its rather frustrating to have the device go flat after applying the gel… price wise, i feel that its slightly pricey but i’m not sure how smile fam compares to other brands. look out for their sales, if you’re interested to try this brand but concerned about their price point! i’d say smile fam is worth a try if you’re unwilling to spend at the dentist (or afraid to go).
