I recently switched to the Smilefam toothbrush, and I'm genuinely impressed. Having previously used an 'ion' toothbrush from Japan, I can say that the Smilefam is an upgrade. Despite that small point, I highly recommend the Smilefam for anyone seeking a powerful and effective electric toothbrush.
