Are the reviews fake?

It is a fair question to ask of any brand-run review page, including this one. Three things you can check yourself. Most of these reviews sit on Google and Facebook, where SmileFam cannot edit or delete them — every source in the table above links to its original listing. The critical reviews are published here in full rather than filtered out. And the per-source counts add up to the stated total, so the arithmetic is checkable.

What this page cannot prove is that every individual reviewer bought the product. Google and Facebook reviews are not purchase-verified, and each one is labelled as such rather than badged “verified”. The methodology page sets out exactly what is collected and what is left out.
